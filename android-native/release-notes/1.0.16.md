# Harbor 1.0.16 for Android

Harbor 1.0.16 makes seeking on phones and tablets look and work like Harbor PC, adds an episode reviews button and episode descriptions in your language, and makes Continue Watching pick up exactly where you left off on any device. Auto sync is now an experimental feature, off by default.

## 📦 Which APK?

- **Harbor_1.0.16_android.apk:** Phones and tablets (64-bit ARM).
- **Harbor_1.0.16_android-armv7.apk:** Android TVs and TV boxes (32-bit).
- **Harbor_1.0.16_android-universal.apk:** Works on any device but is bigger. Use it if you're not sure.

Already on 1.0.15 or older? Harbor offers this update by itself, or go to Settings → Updates → Check for updates.

---

## ✨ Added

**🎬 Player (phones and tablets)**
- **Seeking Like the PC:** Dragging the seek bar now looks like Harbor PC. The bar grows while you drag it, a larger dot follows your finger, and a preview box above it shows the frame at that spot with the time underneath. The rewind, play and forward buttons step aside while you drag.
- **Swipe to Seek:** Swiping sideways on the video now shows the same seek bar and preview instead of numbers in the middle of the screen.
- **Steady Seek Bar:** After you seek, the bar stays on the new spot instead of jumping back for a moment.
- **Audio Menu:** The Audio menu now shows the track even when a video has only one.

**📺 Episodes**
- **Episode Reviews Button:** Each episode now has a reviews button beside it, opening that episode's IMDb and Trakt reviews.
- **Descriptions in Your Language:** Episode descriptions now come from TMDB in your Description language (Settings → App language), so you can read them in Arabic or any other language. Needs a TMDB key.

**⏱️ Auto Sync**
- **Download the Model From the Player:** When auto sync needs the Whisper speech model, the popup now has a Download model button with a progress bar, then Sync now. No trip to Settings.

---

## 🛠️ Changed

- **Auto Sync Is Experimental:** Auto sync is now off by default. Turn it on with Auto sync (Experimental) in Settings → Subtitle timing. Until then, its button and options are hidden.
- **Chapter Marks Off:** The small chapter lines on the seek bar are now off by default, as in the latest Harbor PC. Turn them back on from the player's Chapters menu.

---

## 🐞 Fixed

- **Continue Watching Resumes Exactly:** Playing from Continue Watching now starts at the newest place you reached, on this device or any other (Harbor PC, Stremio), like Harbor PC. When an episode opens, Harbor checks Stremio for your latest place and moves there if it's newer.
- **Continue Watching Stays Up to Date:** Coming back to Harbor now refreshes your Stremio library, so progress from other devices shows without restarting the app.
- **Clearer Auto Sync Errors:** If the speech model is still busy from the last sync, auto sync now says so instead of asking you to download it again.
- **Subtitles With No Gaps:** Auto sync now handles subtitles whose lines run straight into each other.
- **Settings Search:** Searching Settings for "description" now finds the Description language setting.
