# Harbor 1.0.19.1 for Android

Harbor 1.0.19.1 makes trackers sync the way Harbor PC does, brings tracker sign-in and the new title-page options to Android TV, speeds up launch, Home and title pages, and fixes a set of bugs, including two crashes in Community.

---

## ✨ Added

**📺 Android TV**
- **Connect Trakt and Simkl on the TV:** Settings → Setup has Trakt, Simkl, AniList and MyAnimeList pages. Trakt and Simkl show a code, the address and a QR code, and connect as soon as you approve. AniList and MyAnimeList explain how to connect from your phone and send it to the TV.
- **Tracker Tabs in Library:** Trakt, Simkl, AniList, MyAnimeList, Letterboxd and PublicMetaDB each get a tab while connected.
- **Library Menu on Title Pages:** Saving a title also updates your trackers, and on a saved title Save opens Save to library, Add to all trackers, Remove from library, Remove from all trackers and each tracker's status.
- **Up Next Countdown:** Anime episodes that haven't aired show "in 2d 4h" on Continue watching.
- **Which Order and Anime Identity:** The two new settings from phones are now in TV Settings → Home.

**🔎 Discover**
- **Browse by Award:** PC's row of award tiles (Academy Awards, Golden Globes, Cannes and more).

---

## 🛠️ Changed

- **Trackers Work Like Harbor PC:**
  - Trakt and Simkl now count an episode as watched at the same points PC does: 90% when you close the player, while leaving earlier pauses it. Videos under 2.5 minutes aren't sent.
  - The next episode no longer closes the previous one under the wrong id.
  - A watch that couldn't be sent, for example offline, is kept and sent again later.
  - Trakt, Simkl and MyAnimeList renew an expired sign-in by themselves, and a network error no longer signs you out.
  - Simkl gets Harbor's app details and rate limits, as on PC.
  - Anime and TMDB-only titles now reach Trakt and Simkl.
  - AniList and MyAnimeList record an episode at 70% and set a planned show to Watching after 90 seconds.
  - PublicMetaDB marks a finished title watched and clears its resume point itself.
  - Marking episodes or a season by hand now reaches Simkl and PublicMetaDB.
- **Title Page Buttons on One Line:** Play, Save to library, the star and ⋯ sit on one line on phones and tablets. Everything else is in ⋯; on phones it opens as a sheet from the bottom.
- **Discover:** The Calendar, Collections and Awards buttons are gone from the top, as on PC. Collections is in the account menu.
- **Loading a Video:** Only the Connecting screen shows while a source loads (no player controls on top), and it fits a phone held sideways.
- **Faster:**
  - Launch and Home's first screen no longer wait on file reads.
  - Large responses are processed off the main thread, so poster rows scroll more smoothly.
  - Title pages load their extra details at the same time and don't download the same season or metadata twice.
  - Library sorting is quicker.
  - Opening a downloaded video doesn't scan every download.

---

## 🐞 Fixed

- **Community Crashes:** Opening your profile offline, and a sent message appearing twice in a chat, no longer crash the app.
- **Profiles and Harbor Accounts:** Switching profile while a Harbor sign-in renews no longer puts one profile's account on another.
- **Community Friends:** Changing Harbor account no longer shows the previous account's friend buttons.
- **Next Episode Progress:** Moving to the next episode no longer saves the previous episode's position on it.
- **Playing in the Background:** Pressing Home while a video loads no longer starts it playing in the background.
- **Auto Sync:** The Moonshine download prompt is no longer hidden behind an error.
- **DVD and Absolute Order:** Episode descriptions, ratings and dates match the right episode.
- **Cloud Library:** A file for a different show with a longer name (such as "The Office Australia" for "The Office") is no longer picked.
- **Finished-Episode Sync:** A finish that failed to reach Stremio or PublicMetaDB is retried, and no longer blocks another profile.

## 📦 Which APK?

- **Harbor_1.0.19.1_android.apk:** Phones and tablets (64-bit ARM).
- **Harbor_1.0.19.1_android-armv7.apk:** Android TVs and TV boxes (32-bit).
- **Harbor_1.0.19.1_android-universal.apk:** Works on any device but is bigger. Use it if you're not sure.

Already on 1.0.19 or older? Harbor offers this update by itself, or go to Settings → Updates → Check for updates.
