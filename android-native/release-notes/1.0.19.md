# Harbor 1.0.19 for Android

Harbor 1.0.19 brings Harbor PC's Community and its new title-page buttons to Android, adds an automatic player engine, audio passthrough and the Moonshine speech model, and makes Trakt, Simkl and MyAnimeList connect with one tap. It also fixes Trakt stopping after a while and the Trakt and Letterboxd library tabs staying empty.

---

## ✨ Added

**👥 Community**
- **Community Page Like Harbor PC:** People with match filters, Friends (online now and offline), Messages and Invites, plus your own profile card to edit. Open it from the account menu, or the rail on tablets.
- **Member Reviews on Title Pages:** Read what Harbor members thought of a title, like a review and read its replies.
- **Send to a Friend:** Share a title with a friend straight from its page.

**🎬 Title Pages**
- **New Button Row Like Harbor PC:** Save to library with a menu (Save to library, Add to all trackers, Remove from library, Remove from all trackers), Rate, and a ⋯ menu with Favorite, Add to list, Add to queue, Mark watched, Watch trailer and Download for offline.
- **Your Trackers in ⋯:** When the row runs out of room, each connected tracker gets its own rows, so you can add a title to just one of them. Trakt adds Add to / Remove from Trakt watchlist and Mark watched on Trakt.
- **Episode Order:** Settings → Detail pages → Which order numbers episodes by TVDB's Aired, Official, DVD, Absolute or Alternate order (needs your TVDB key). Playback, watched marks and trackers keep the aired episodes.
- **Episode Pages:** Seasons with more than 50 episodes are split into pages, on title pages and in the player.

**▶️ Player**
- **Automatic Player Engine:** Anime and ASS subtitles play in mpv, everything else in Android's player, and a video that won't play in one switches to the other at the same position.
- **Audio Passthrough:** Send Dolby Digital, Dolby Digital Plus, Atmos, TrueHD and DTS to your TV, soundbar or receiver. Off by default, with a switch for each format.
- **Your Own Seek and Speed Steps:** Choose how far back/forward, long seek and speed steps go.
- **Skip Button Progress:** The Skip Intro, Recap and Credits buttons fill up as the segment plays, as on Harbor PC.
- **Browse Anime Cours:** The player's Episodes panel moves between the cours of a split anime (e.g. Bleach TYBW).
- **Keyboard Shortcuts:** D cycles audio tracks; Shift+[ and Shift+] change the subtitle size.

**💬 Subtitle Sync**
- **Moonshine Speech Model:** A faster speech model for Auto sync, picked by the audio's language, with its own download and Remove button in Subtitle timing.
- **Silero Speech Detection:** Auto sync finds speech more accurately before it lines subtitles up.

**📚 Continue Watching and Library**
- **Anime Up Next:** After you finish an anime episode, the next one appears in Continue watching once AniList lists it, with a countdown ("in 2d 4h") until it airs.
- **Also Clear Provider Progress:** An option under Settings → Home that also deletes a dismissed card's saved position on Trakt, Simkl and PublicMetaDB. Off by default; watch history and watchlists are never touched.
- **Hide Library Tabs:** Library → Tabs chooses which tabs show.
- **Debrid Cloud Libraries as Sources:** Files already in your TorBox, Real-Debrid, AllDebrid and other debrid clouds show up as sources.
- **Refract:** Sends your watched movies and episodes, and watchlist changes, to Refract.

**🎌 Anime**
- **AniList Air Dates:** Anime pages and the Library calendar use AniList's airing schedule, and the monthly calendar now reads busy months in full.
- **Offline Anime Identity Database (Experimental):** Settings → Home can download a map of which Kitsu, MyAnimeList, AniList, AniDB and Simkl entries are the same anime, to join duplicate Continue watching cards and check tracker targets before syncing. Off by default.

**🔎 Sources**
- **Largest, Smallest and Most Seeders:** New source orders.
- **YouTube Sources:** Addon sources that are YouTube videos play inside Harbor.
- **Stream Priority Card:** Shows how your stream addons are ordered, as on Harbor PC.

**🌍 Languages**
- **Right-to-Left Layout:** Arabic lays Harbor out right to left.

## 🛠️ Changed

- **One-Tap Tracker Sign-In:** Trakt, Simkl and MyAnimeList connect with Harbor's own apps, as on Harbor PC, with no developer app to set up. Your own app is still available.
- **Loading Screen Like Harbor PC:** While a source connects you see the title's art, its logo, Harbor's sailing boat, CONNECTING and Cancel, with no flash of the source list before the player opens.
- **Menus Like Harbor PC:** The title page's ⋯ and Save to library menus use Harbor PC's icons, order and style.
- **Season Picker Beside Episodes:** On phones the season menu sits next to the Episodes heading instead of at the far end of the toolbar.
- **Letterboxd Library Tab:** Shows your Letterboxd watchlist, as on Harbor PC, whichever lists Home shows.
- **Content Advisory Removed From Title Pages:** As on Harbor PC. The player's advisory card is unchanged.
- **Remembered Sources Always On:** Autoplay prefers the release you picked before, and the next episode keeps your addon, quality, release group and dub.
- **Search:** Keeps its results when you come back, with TMDB posters.
- **AIOStreams:** Gets at least 60 seconds to answer.

---

## 🐞 Fixed

- **Trakt Stopping After a While:** Trakt's sign-in renewal could be lost, which left Trakt failing for good. Renewals are now kept and run one at a time. If Trakt says your sign-in has expired, connect it again once in Settings → Trakt.
- **Empty Trakt and Letterboxd Tabs:** Library's tracker tabs now load, and say why when they can't instead of "Nothing here yet".
- **Wrong Episode Dates:** Shows whose addon gives every episode one placeholder date (such as The Office) now use TMDB's air dates.
- **"0m Left":** Continue watching shows "1m left" in the last minute.
- **Box on the Home Button:** The tablet rail no longer draws a box round Home after a key press.
- **PublicMetaDB:** An episode is no longer marked watched twice.
- **Resume Near the End:** A Stremio position at the very end of a video no longer resumes there.
- **Subtitles for Movies:** Subtitle search no longer sends a season and episode for films.
- **Addon Requests:** Addon settings in the manifest address are kept, and an addon that's rate-limited waits before trying again.
- **Source Sizes:** Read correctly from more addons, so size badges, filters and ordering agree.
- **List Addresses:** MDBList and Trakt list links with accents, dots or tildes work.

## 📦 Which APK?

- **Harbor_1.0.19_android.apk:** Phones and tablets (64-bit ARM).
- **Harbor_1.0.19_android-armv7.apk:** Android TVs and TV boxes (32-bit).
- **Harbor_1.0.19_android-universal.apk:** Works on any device but is bigger. Use it if you're not sure.

Already on 1.0.18 or older? Harbor offers this update by itself, or go to Settings → Updates → Check for updates.
