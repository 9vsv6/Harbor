# Harbor 1.0.17 for Android

Harbor 1.0.17 makes Android TV faster and smoother, puts the sources your TV can play smoothly first, adds a subtitle delay bar you can use while watching, and fixes a long list of TV glitches. Auto sync can now sync subtitles in another language than the audio, and works on more devices.

## 📦 Which APK?

- **Harbor_1.0.17_android.apk:** Phones and tablets (64-bit ARM).
- **Harbor_1.0.17_android-armv7.apk:** Android TVs and TV boxes (32-bit).
- **Harbor_1.0.17_android-universal.apk:** Works on any device but is bigger. Use it if you're not sure.

Already on 1.0.16 or older? Harbor offers this update by itself, or go to Settings → Updates → Check for updates.

---

## ✨ Added

**📺 Android TV**
- **Sources That Play Smoothly First:** On a TV, the sources list now puts first the files your TV can actually play: no 4K on a Full HD screen, and nothing bigger than your connection carries. Nothing is hidden; the rest follow below. Choose Settings → Sources → Put first → Best quality to keep 4K on top.
- **Too-Heavy Source Notice:** When a source keeps stopping to buffer because it's bigger than your connection, the player says so and suggests a smaller 1080p one.

**🎬 Subtitles**
- **Adjust While Watching (TV):** A new subtitle delay bar at the bottom of the screen, with the film still playing above it. It has buttons to skip back or forward 3 seconds and to play or pause, so you can hear a line again, plus the delay steps (0.1 s and 1 s). Open it from Subtitles → Sync → Adjust while watching.
- **Live Sync Skips:** Live sync on TV now has 3-second back and forward buttons beside Play.
- **Auto Sync for Other Languages:** Auto sync can now sync a subtitle in another language than the audio, such as Arabic subtitles on an English film. It translates the subtitle lines and matches them to the words it hears.
- **One Subtitles Page in TV Settings:** Subtitle languages, appearance and timing are now together on one Subtitles page.

---

## 🛠️ Changed

- **Faster TV Screens:** Big Picture, the title page and Settings draw less on every frame, and the TV code comes precompiled with the app, so moving around is quicker, most of all on 32-bit TV boxes.
- **Settings Opens Faster:** TV Settings no longer stutters for the first seconds after you open it.
- **Lighter Auto Sync on TVs:** Auto sync's audio work now runs at background priority, so playback stays smooth while it listens. On slow 32-bit TVs, where speech recognition is too slow, it syncs by when people speak.

---

## 🐞 Fixed

- **Auto Sync on Android 9 TVs:** Auto sync couldn't read a video's audio on many Android 9 TVs. It now can.
- **Subtitle for the Wrong Episode:** Harbor no longer picks a subtitle by itself when its name says it's for another episode (for example an S01E01 subtitle on episode 2).
- **Up in TV Settings:** After scrolling down a Settings page, Up now goes back through the settings above instead of jumping to the top bar, and the page shows its top again.
- **Leaving Sources:** Going back from Sources to the title page now shows the page at once, without a gap or a flash of the filters.
- **Switching Sections:** Moving from Discover to another section no longer flashes Discover full screen, and the big backdrop no longer shows the last section's title after you switch.
- **TV Home Focus:** After finishing the last item in Continue Watching, coming back to Home now focuses the nearest card.
- **TV Search:** A new search no longer briefly shows "search failed", and its loading spinner no longer disappears early.
- **Your Own Seeks Win:** Seeking yourself right after an episode opens is no longer undone by the Stremio resume.
- **Interrupted Swipe:** An interrupted swipe-to-seek no longer leaves the seek bar stuck.
- **Paused Stays Paused:** If you pause while auto sync is listening, the film stays paused when it finishes.
- **Title Page Refreshes:** A title page you come back to now refreshes, so new episodes show without restarting the app.
- **Auto Sync Errors:** Clearer messages when a source won't share its audio or the video is still loading, and the speech model popup no longer covers other errors.
