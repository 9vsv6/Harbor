# Harbor 1.0.18 for Android

Harbor 1.0.18 makes video smooth on phones and tablets, adds on/off switches for addons, brings the curated lists and Stremio's library orders over from Harbor PC, keeps the phone layout when you turn your phone sideways, and fixes a long list of player bugs. Auto sync can now sync against English subtitles in seconds, which works on TVs too.

---

## ✨ Added

**🧩 Addons**
- **Turn Addons On or Off:** Every installed addon has a switch, as on Harbor PC. An addon that's off stays installed but isn't asked for catalogs, streams or subtitles. The switch is kept on this device.
- **Installed Tab Like Harbor PC:** Numbered cards in two columns on wide screens, with the switch, Manage and Installed, and Reorder to move them.

**🎬 Subtitles and Sync**
- **Auto Sync Against English Subtitles:** Auto sync first compares your subtitle with an English one for the same film and lines them up in seconds, without listening to the audio. It works on TVs too. If that doesn't work, it listens as before.
- **Subtitles Matched to the Release:** Subtitles made for the exact file you're playing are marked "Matches this source" and chosen first.
- **Always Use the Speech Model:** A new option under Subtitle timing → Smart resync skips the quick checks and always listens to the audio.
- **Subtitle Ads Removed:** Lines like "Advertise your product or brand here" and subtitle-site addresses are taken out of downloaded subtitles.

**📚 Library and Lists**
- **The Canon:** The curated lists are now "The canon", with tiles and list pages like Harbor PC: the list's backdrops, the curator's own logo (Sight and Sound, Criterion, AFI and more) and the title count.
- **Stremio's Library Orders:** Last watched, Name, Name descending, Times watched, Watched and Not watched, plus Year.

**🎌 Anime**
- **Anime in Your Language:** Anime posters, titles, descriptions and logos now follow your poster and description languages, as other titles do.

---

## 🛠️ Changed

- **Smooth Video on Phones and Tablets:** The screen now switches to the video's frame rate, so 24 fps films and anime no longer stutter on 60 Hz screens. Screens with no 24 Hz mode play 24 fps at 48 Hz without uneven frames, and faster playback speeds show every frame.
- **Bigger Playback Buffer:** Phones with 6 GB of memory or more keep more video ready, so high-bitrate files ride out network dips.
- **Seek From the Seek Bar Only:** Swiping sideways over the video no longer seeks.
- **Phones Stay Phones Sideways:** Turning your phone sideways keeps the phone layout instead of switching to the tablet one. Home, title pages, Settings and the profile menu fit a sideways screen.
- **Filters Wrap:** Filter and tab rows go onto a new line instead of scrolling sideways (Addons, Sources, Collections, Library).
- **Phone Tab Order:** Home, Discover, Movies, Shows, Anime, in the tablet rail's order.
- **Player Menus in Landscape:** The Audio and Subtitles menus use the full height on a phone held sideways and open at the track that's playing.
- **Instant Source List in the Player:** Sources are kept for 5 minutes, so switching source in the player is instant.
- **Loading Backdrop:** While a source connects, the player shows the title's backdrop instead of black.

---

## 🐞 Fixed

- **Title Page Flash:** Opening a title no longer shows the addon's page for a moment before the TMDB one.
- **Media3 Fallback on Phones:** A file Media3 can't decode now switches to mpv instead of failing.
- **Audio Focus:** Other apps' sound and unplugged headphones now pause the player.
- **Play/Pause While Loading:** Pressing Play/Pause while a video loads now pauses it.
- **Casting:** Your place is saved from the TV, downloaded files cast their online source, and the phone no longer starts playing in the background when casting ends.
- **Subtitle Names:** SubSense and SubDL subtitles show their file name and source instead of a database path.
- **Skip Intro Button:** Readable on bright scenes.
- **Top Button on Sources:** No longer covers the Play button.
- **Library Grouping:** No more "This week / This month" groups from watch dates that differ between devices, and no single "Unknown" year group.
- **Nuvio and MyAnimeList:** A network error no longer signs you out.
- **Debrid:** Real-Debrid and AllDebrid torrents are removed when a source fails or you leave the page.
- **Extensions:** Only installed over https, with a size limit.

## 📦 Which APK?

- **Harbor_1.0.18_android.apk:** Phones and tablets (64-bit ARM).
- **Harbor_1.0.18_android-armv7.apk:** Android TVs and TV boxes (32-bit).
- **Harbor_1.0.18_android-universal.apk:** Works on any device but is bigger. Use it if you're not sure.

Already on 1.0.17 or older? Harbor offers this update by itself, or go to Settings → Updates → Check for updates.
